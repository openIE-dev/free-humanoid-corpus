## Backpropagation (1986-10)

- **id**: `backpropagation-rumelhart-hinton-1986`
- **corpus**: academic
- **creator**: Carnegie Mellon University + UCSD; David E. Rumelhart, Geoffrey Hinton, Ronald Williams
- **disclosure**: Rumelhart, D. E., Hinton, G. E., Williams, R. J. 'Learning Representations by Back-Propagating Errors'. Nature 323 1986. Antecedent: Werbos, P. PhD thesis Harvard 1974 (the mathematical foundations); Linnainmaa, S. master's thesis Helsinki 1970 (chain-rule application to neural networks).
- **ip status**: public-domain
- **prior art notes**: Backpropagation (Rumelhart-Hinton-Williams Nature 1986; antecedents Werbos 1974, Linnainmaa 1970) is the foundational neural-network training algorithm. 39-year-deep public-domain prior art. The substrate of every neural network in the corpus, every VLA, every policy. Direct shielding for any commercial humanoid claim using neural networks (which is essentially every modern humanoid system).

## Dropout regularization (2014-06)

- **id**: `dropout-srivastava-jmlr-2014`
- **corpus**: academic
- **creator**: University of Toronto; Nitish Srivastava, Geoffrey Hinton, Alex Krizhevsky, Ilya Sutskever, Ruslan Salakhutdinov
- **disclosure**: Srivastava, N., Hinton, G., Krizhevsky, A., Sutskever, I., Salakhutdinov, R. 'Dropout: A Simple Way to Prevent Neural Networks from Overfitting'. Journal of Machine Learning Research 15 2014. University of Toronto.
- **ip status**: public-domain
- **prior art notes**: Dropout (Srivastava + Hinton et al. JMLR 2014) is the canonical neural-network regularization technique. 11-year-deep public-domain prior art. Used in essentially every deep neural network in the corpus.
