## Trieste bathyscaphe (first crewed Challenger Deep dive) (1953-08)

- **id**: `trieste-bathyscaphe-piccard-1953`
- **corpus**: academic
- **creator**: Auguste + Jacques Piccard (Switzerland/Belgium); built in Italy; later US Navy
- **disclosure**: Piccard, A. + Piccard, J. Trieste bathyscaphe constructed 1953, Castellammare di Stabia, Italy. Reached Challenger Deep (10,916 m) on January 23, 1960 with Don Walsh + Jacques Piccard aboard. The first crewed vessel to the deepest point on Earth. Acquired by US Navy 1958.
- **ip status**: public-domain
- **prior art notes**: Trieste bathyscaphe (Piccard 1953-1960) is the first crewed vessel to the deepest point on Earth. 72-year-deep public-domain prior art.
