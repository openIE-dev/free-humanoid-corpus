## Lagrangian Mechanics (Joseph-Louis Lagrange 1788; foundation of robot dynamics) (1788-01)

- **disclosure**: Lagrange, J.-L. 'Mécanique Analytique'. Paris, 1788. Reformulated Newtonian mechanics in terms of generalized coordinates + the Lagrangian L = T - V (kinetic minus potential energy) + the principle of least action.
