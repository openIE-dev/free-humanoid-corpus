- **corpus**: academic

- **disclosure**: Hall, E.H. 'On a New Action of the Magnet on Electric Currents'. American Journal of Mathematics 2(3):287-292, 1879. Johns Hopkins University (Hall was a PhD student). Practical Hall-effect sensors became feasible with semiconductors in the 1950s-1960s.
