## PID Control (Nicolas Minorsky 1922; ship autopilot) (1922-01)

- **id**: `minorsky-pid-control-1922`
- **corpus**: academic
- **creator**: US Navy; Nicolas Minorsky
- **disclosure**: Minorsky, N. 'Directional stability of automatically steered bodies'. Journal of the American Society of Naval Engineers 34(2):280-309, 1922. US Navy. Derived from observing how a helmsman steers a ship — the first theoretical treatment of three-term (PID) control.
- **ip status**: public-domain
- **prior art notes**: PID Control (Nicolas Minorsky US Navy 1922) is the foundational three-term feedback controller. 103-year-deep public-domain prior art. The single most-deployed control algorithm in history.

- **creator**: Taylor Instrument Companies (Rochester, NY, USA); John G. Ziegler + Nathaniel B. Nichols
- **disclosure**: Ziegler, J.G., Nichols, N.B. 'Optimum Settings for Automatic Controllers'. Transactions of the ASME 64:759-768, November 1942. Taylor Instrument Companies (Rochester, NY, USA).

- **prior art notes**: Ziegler-Nichols PID Tuning Rules (Ziegler & Nichols Taylor Instrument Companies ASME 1942) are the foundational practical method for tuning PID controllers. 83-year-deep public-domain prior art. Made PID (corpus minorsky-pid-control-1922) universally deployable.
