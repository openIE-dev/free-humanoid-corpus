## Variational Autoencoder (VAE) (2013-12)

- **id**: `vae-kingma-iclr-2014`
- **corpus**: academic
- **creator**: University of Amsterdam; Diederik P. Kingma, Max Welling
- **disclosure**: Kingma, D. P., Welling, M. 'Auto-Encoding Variational Bayes'. ICLR 2014. arXiv:1312.6114. University of Amsterdam.

- **creator**: UC Berkeley; Jonathan Ho, Ajay Jain, Pieter Abbeel; antecedent: Stanford Sohl-Dickstein 2015

Direct shielding for any commercial humanoid claim on diffusion-based action generation. Closes a major foundational citation chain.
