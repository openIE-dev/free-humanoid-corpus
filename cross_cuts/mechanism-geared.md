## Antikythera Mechanism (~150-100 BCE; ancient analog computer) (2006-11)

- **id**: `antikythera-mechanism-150bce`
- **corpus**: academic
- **creator**: Hellenistic Greek artisan(s) (~150-100 BCE; possibly Rhodes / Hipparchus school)
- **disclosure**: Built ~150-100 BCE (Hellenistic Greece, possibly Rhodes). Recovered 1901 from a shipwreck off Antikythera island. First detailed analysis: Freeth, T. et al. 'Decoding the ancient Greek astronomical calculator known as the Antikythera Mechanism'. Nature 444:587-591, November 2006.
- **ip status**: public-domain
- **prior art notes**: The Antikythera Mechanism (~150-100 BCE Hellenistic Greece) is the oldest known geared analog computer. 2,000+-year-deep public-domain prior art. The conceptual ancestor of all geared mechanism + clockwork automation.
