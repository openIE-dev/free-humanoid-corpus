## Open Invention Network (2005-11)

- **id**: `open-invention-network-2005`
- **corpus**: open
- **creator**: IBM + Sony + Philips + Red Hat + NEC + Novell (founding consortium)
- **disclosure**: Open Invention Network LLC. Founded November 2005 by IBM, Sony, Philips, Red Hat, NEC, and Novell as a defensive patent pool for the Linux ecosystem. openinventionnetwork.com. Subsequent expansion to 4,000+ licensees including Google, Microsoft, Toyota, and most major tech companies.
- **ip status**: open-permissive
- **prior art notes**: Open Invention Network is the canonical defensive-patent-pool institution for open-source software (founded November 2005). 20-year operational record. Establishes the architectural pattern of: cross-licensed defensive patent pool, open-source-licensee indemnification, strategic patent-acquisition fund. **Directly relevant to the free-humanoid-corpus mission**: the corpus IS a defensive-publication commons for humanoid robotics. OIN is the institutional analogue in the patent space (we publish prior art rather than pool patents); both serve to neutralize offensive IP threats to open work. Models for how a corpus-based commons could federate with a patent-pool commons.
