## Stanford Arm (Scheinman 1969) (1969-06)

- **id**: `stanford-arm-scheinman-1969`
- **corpus**: academic
- **creator**: Stanford AI Laboratory; Victor Scheinman
- **disclosure**: Scheinman, V.D. 'Design of a Computer Controlled Manipulator'. Stanford AI Memo 92, June 1969. Stanford Artificial Intelligence Laboratory. Subsequently commercialized as Vicarm (Scheinman's company), then sold to Unimation.
- **ip status**: public-domain
- **prior art notes**: Stanford Arm (Scheinman Stanford AI Lab 1969) is the foundational all-electric 6-axis arm with closed-form kinematics. 56-year-deep public-domain prior art. Direct ancestor of PUMA (round-45) and every modern 6-DoF industrial arm.
