### 2009-04 — Roetenberg Inertial Motion Capture (Xsens MVN)

- **id:** `roetenberg-xsens-imu`
- **corpus:** academic
- **ip status:** open-permissive
- **creator:** Daniel Roetenberg, Henk Luinge, Per Slycke; Xsens Technologies (Enschede, Netherlands) + University of Twente
- **disclosure citation:** Roetenberg, Daniel, Luinge, Henk, Slycke, Per. 'Xsens MVN: Full 6DOF human motion tracking using miniature inertial sensors.' Xsens Technologies White Paper, April 2009. Foundational thesis: Roetenberg, D. 'Inertial and Magnetic Sensing of Human Motion.' PhD Thesis, University of Twente, May 2006.
- **disclosed subsystems:** `sensing-imu`

**Prior art notes:**

> Roetenberg's Xsens MVN system is the canonical academic + commercial disclosure of full-body inertial motion capture without external infrastructure. Anticipates: (1) the integration of multiple body-mounted IMUs with a biomechanical skeleton model for drift-bounded human pose tracking — directly relevant to claims on humanoid motion-capture-driven control (Tesla Optimus operator suit, Figure operator data collection, every teleop-suit humanoid program); (2) the EKF-based per-IMU orientation fusion with magnetometer correction — relevant to humanoid IMU-fusion IP; (3) zero-velocity foot-contact updates for drift correction — relevant to humanoid proprioceptive-fusion IP. Roetenberg PhD thesis publicly available; Xsens MVN white paper (2009) publicly distributed. Modern humanoid motion-capture-control patents face this 17-year-deep 102 anchor.

**Sources:**

1. Roetenberg, D., Luinge, H., Slycke, P. 'Xsens MVN.' Xsens White Paper, 2009.
2. Roetenberg, D. PhD Thesis: 'Inertial and Magnetic Sensing of Human Motion.' University of Twente, 2006.
